# Interact speeches — public speaking and training

Recordings from my term as **Protocol Officer at Interact Club Ruspina Monastir
(2023–2025)**, a club of 600+ members.

Protocol Officer is the role responsible for ceremony, conduct and formal
proceedings at club events — opening and closing sessions, introducing guests,
and keeping meetings to their form. Most of what follows is that work, plus the
training sessions I ran for other members.

system the club runs on, then presented it to the members who would use it.

## Why this repository exists

Technical work is easy to evidence; speaking and teaching are not. These are the
raw recordings rather than a highlight reel: they show how I explain something to
a room, in Arabic, French and English, without editing.

The filename `prtocole-speech.mp4` carries a typo from the original upload. It is
left as-is so existing links keep working.
